## Full description – English (max 4000)

```
Asta Chemma Traditional (అష్ట చెమ్మ) is the traditional board game played in Telugu homes with cowrie shells and a chalk-drawn 5×5 board – now made for your phone, with the true house rules.

HOW IT PLAYS
• Throw five cowrie shells and count how many land open: 1, 2, 3, 4 or 8.
• Bring your Mother and two Children from your starting area round the outer ring, through the inner ring and home to the lotus in the centre.
• A 4 or an 8 earns another throw – keep it for later, or join two 4s to release your Mother.
• Capture like for like – a Child takes a Child, a Mother takes a Mother – and throw again. The X squares are safe.
• First to bring all three pieces home wins. Play in teams of two, too.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Against the computer – easy, medium or hard.
• Pass & Play – family and friends on one phone, 2 to 4 players or 2 v 2 teams.
• Online with friends – create a room, share the 4-letter code and play together on your own phones. Everyone sees their own side at the bottom. Lost your connection? Rejoin and carry on – the computer keeps your seat warm.
• Quick match – no code needed: tap "Find a player" and play someone online right away.

LEARN AS YOU PLAY
New to Asta Chemma? A short guided game teaches every rule as you play, and How to play explains it all with pictures.

COINS, SHOP AND LEADERBOARD
Collect a daily bonus and win coins online, then spend them on new boards, marbles and cowrie styles. Climb the weekly leaderboard of wins against friends. Coins are just for fun – they can't be bought, sold or exchanged for money.

MADE WITH CARE
• English and Telugu throughout
• Shape markers for colour-blind players
• Sounds, game speed and auto-move settings
• Undo in games against the computer
• Sign in with Google to keep your coins and stats on any phone – or just play as a guest
• No ads. No tracking.
```
